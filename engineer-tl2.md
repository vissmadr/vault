• The Engineer has 30 class skills: 21 actives and 9 passives. Tier bonuses unlock at ranks 5 /
  10 / 15. The Charge bar has five segments; below, “per Charge” means per filled segment.

  Blitz passives

  - Heavy Lifting: with a two-handed greataxe, greatsword, greathammer, polearm, or cannon, each
    rank grants +2% attack speed and +2% chance to stun for 3 seconds; max rank is +30% to both.

  - Supercharge: melee hits can activate a 10-second supercharged state. While active, normal
    melee attacks gain an extra hit and normal attacks plus Onslaught gain more Charge. It
    scales from a 25% extra hit / 3% extra Charge at rank 1 to 95% / 45% at rank 15.

  - Coup-de-Grace: hitting a stunned enemy deals an extra electric hit, at most once per second.
    Damage is based chiefly on Strength: 3× Strength at rank 1, increasing by 0.5× Strength per
    rank to 10× at rank 15.

  Construction passives

  - Bulwark: raises physical armor by 2% per rank, up to 30%, and reduces physical damage by 2%
    at rank 1 through 16% at rank 15.

  - Fire and Spark: +5% fire and electric damage per rank, up to +75% each. It does not improve
    minion damage.

  - Charge Domination: on killing an enemy, a 3%–10% chance to fill the entire Charge bar;
    cannot trigger more than once every 3 seconds.

  Aegis passives

  - Sword and Board: when wielding a shield and melee weapon, adds 20%–90% of shield armor to
    melee attacks as physical damage.

  - Aegis of Fate: when hit, has a 2%–16% chance to create a barrier worth 200% of armor + 100,
    with 50% knockback resistance while active.

  - Charge Reconstitution: whenever a skill spends Charge, recover health over 2 seconds. It
    scales from 533 at rank 1 to 1,758 at rank 15 at character level 100; spending several
    segments at once does not stack the healing.
