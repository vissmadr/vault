## Drills

**Olympic Roll**: From front-headlock, choking arm passes through, supporting arm catches it in a Gable-grip.
. Will call this grip the "short-anaconda" grip.
. Squeeze your forearms in order to keep his elbow inside.
. Put your shoulder and head low so that your upper-body supports the structure.
. On your feet, start to lean to the side. This will already apply a very strong choke.
. If he is going to roll, your head should naturally be below him for an olympic-roll.

**Reverse Olympic Roll**: From the same front-headlock short-anaconda grip, the idea is to roll the guy the other way.
. It's usually the "wrong" way because he has a hand there that he can post on.
. The idea is to trap his head underneath our stomach, and when we roll it's going to be like a Nelson mechanic.
