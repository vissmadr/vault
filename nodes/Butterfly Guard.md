# Butterfly Guard

Inside position grappling guard.

---

Butterfly is a versatile guard that offers many attack opportunities.

Multiple good upper-body connections:
- Overhook
- Collartie
- Underhook variations

Butterfly guard and Half-butterfly allows for easy side switching.
This is useful as you can attack and adapt to both sides and surprise the opponent.

## Handfight

People often forget about handfighting in general, but it is very important.

**Hands Out**: Hands out to gripfight, protecting the legs.
. Without the hands, the legs are vulenrable to access.

**Climbing Control**: Good control usually happens progressively step-by-step.
. Go from one control to another, progressing the chain.
. Big long-range motions can get countered easily.

**Dominant Out-Under Grip**: The Out-Under grip offers great attack opportunities.
. The first good grip from engage distance.
. Can also push back with those grips to create distance and reset.
. Distance management can create new openings, such as Shin guard entry.

### Two-On-One Control

One hand grips the wrist, the other grips the tricep just above the elbow.

When the opponent's arm is lifted, you grab the wrist from below.
When the opponent's arm is down, you grab the wrist from above.

The Two-On-One grip can push, pull, and expose on the outside.

With a standing opponent, a Two-On-One pull can make him pull back, exposing his legs.

The Two-On-One grip really opens up the game with good control and many opportunities.

## Double Shin Sweep

When a standing opponent is in front with his feet in a perpendicular line.
Open the space, get in with both shins, gripping behind his knees, then grip both feet, sweep.
After the sweep, go for Ashi or stand up for top position.

## Inside Shin Sweep

From Half-butterfly, when the opponent posts on his leg.
Grab the foot, put the leg hook inside, and pull your hips so you're centered and closer to him.
From this position, lift his foot in the air, push back with the shin, and pull the foot hard.

## Marcelo Split Sweep

Against a standing opponent, get any Two-On-One grip and pull, expecting him to pull back.
When he pulls back, scoot in, and sweep him from behind with one leg, while pushing his knee with your arm.

## Basic Ashi Entry

When a standing opponent is in front with his feet in a perpendicular line.
Open the space, get in, gripping behind his knees, and shoot for the ashi.
Hips need to shoot up and be in the air.
One leg bites over, the other has its foot behind his leg.

## Forcing Standing Opponent to Knees

Dragging standing opponents to their knees so you can progress seated butterfly controls.

With any upper-body grip against a standing opponent, shrimp back and pull down.

**Interlocked Fingers Grip**: Especially effective grip to drag people down.
