# Capybaras and Birds

There are birds who perch on capybaras and eat ticks, flies, and other parasites from their fur.
The birds gain an easy meal, while the capybaras receive grooming and relief from irritating and potentially disease-carrying parasites.

Birds may also follow grazing capybaras to catch insects disturbed by their movement.

The birds have keen eyes and lightning reactions.
They can spot a predator, such as the [[Jaguar]], and blow his cover, allowing the capybaras to escape.
