# Clock Theory (Grappling)

Way to think about posture in grappling.

---

From Referee position, imagine a clock on the ground, as if describing a circle around the player, where the configuration is like:
- 12 o'clock is at the head.
- 6 o'clock is at the tail.
- 3 o'clock is to the right.
- 9 o'clock is to the left.

We can split the circle into quadrants.
The human body in that position is naturally effective in covering these quadrants, such that:
- The right hand covers from 12 to 3.
- The right leg covers from 3 to 6.
- The left leg covers from 6 to 9.
- The left hand covers from 9 to 12.

Using the limbs to cover other quadrants they aren't supposed to exposes openings to fall down.
