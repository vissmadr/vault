# Current Timeline Tech Differences

The current age that we live in (`2026-10`) has some ridiculous technology differences between people.

It's like you're playing an old-school RTS game where one nation is still fighting with swords and spears, while another nation is already researching nanotechnology.

This also applies to the economy.
I don't think there was a time in history where the difference between the poor and the richest was that drastic.

There are people right now that can single-handedly change the economies of countries, are literally richer than some countries combined, can afford to travel 24/7 on private jets, and so on.
Simultaneously there are people that are still struggling to build shelter and access clean water.

It's weird to me because the world is open right now, and has been that way since about a generation.
You can connect and talk to anyone in the world in real time now.
Almost all of technology and human knowledge is accessible on the internet by anyone.

Even so, there are communities that are living the same lifestyle as they would have lived 100 years ago.
Actually, wrong, people had a ton of great technology 100 years ago that the above-mentioned communities haven't developed yet.

You have illiterate people that are breaking their backs working on farmland to survive and somehow barely feed their many children, while you have others flying over them on airplanes, who are thinking about things such as the new TV series they are watching and how the development of AI is affecting the economy.

My timeline is insane.
