# Design

The intentional selection of a configuration from a space of possible configurations.

---

Design is usually a very subjective matter.

A designer is a person who intentionally chooses and specifies the structure, behavior, properties, and form of something in order to satisfy some given requirements and constraints.

The design is not the implementation:
- **Design**: Specifies what the thing should be.
- **Implementation**: Realizes that specification.
