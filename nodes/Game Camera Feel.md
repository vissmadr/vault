# Game Camera Feel

Game feel techniques related to the camera and its movement.

---

**Smooth Follow**: Can follow a character by smoothly lagging behind him. Experiment with different interpolation techniques.

**Deadband**: Hidden radius/region of tolerated range before the camera movement responds. Useful for preventing constant tiny camera adjustments.

**Directional Impulse**: Movement towards a direction of interest. For example in a top-down scenario with a player attack, to kick the camera in the direction of the attack.

**Screen Shake**: Self-explanatory. See [[Screen Shake]]
