# Game Camera

Viewpoint through which the player sees the game world.

---

A game camera controls what is visible and how the scene is framed.

**Movement**: Depending on the game, the camera can move in various ways. It could be controlled by the player, follow a character, jump to events, remain fixed, move independently, and so on.

**2D**: Usually controls position, rotation, and zoom within the game world.

**3D**: Also controls orientation, projection, and field of view.

See [[Game Camera Movement]].
