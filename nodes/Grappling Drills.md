# Grappling Drills

List of grappling drills.

Intentionally without much detail, instead focusing on having a large list of drills.

## Balance and Pressure

**Chest to Back**: Opponent stays in turtle.
. Hands behind your back.
. Chest to back connection to your turtled opponent.
. Run in circles around him, maintaining the connection.

## Knee Cut (Top)

**Anaconda Hunt**: When you think you can connect the circle on him.
. Connect the circle, jump to the side, hunt for the choke.
. Try to flow to other dominant positions if the choke is defended.

### Countering the Frame

**Push-pull to Arm Triangle**: When he pushes you over the shoulder.
. Weight on the frame, time his push, pull back, catch him behind his elbow, put your head behind his elbow, drop, crossface, connect the hands.

**Push-pull to Rau Drag**: When he pushes you over the shoulder.
. Weight on the frame, time his push, pull back, catch his wrist and an underhook, drop.

**Push-pull to Darce**: When he pushes you with his hand in the armpit.
. Weight on the frame, time his push, pull his elbow with the crossface arm, turn to the side and shoot for a darce.

## Diagonal Headlock (Top)

**To Turtle Control**: Conservative play.
. Weight on the opponent, knee inside the pocket, switch from Circle to Seatbelt, far Dagestan grip.

**Peruvian Necktie**: When you have a solid grip on the Circle.
. Tighten the Circle, step and put your knee above his head, push his head down, leg over his head, other leg over his back, deadlift.

## Turtle Control (Top)

**Crucifix Arm Trap**: From Turtle Control with a knee inside and a Dagestan grip.
. Push his elbow out with your knee, catch with the other leg.
. Stay on top of the Turtle Control and forcefully choke with one hand against no defense.
