## Leg Bite

Pull the opponent in with your knee shield and angle him away towards your back.
Your bottom leg (half guard leg) can bite his leg from the outside.
Use your arms to support all of this too.
With the outside bite, you can transition to any form of [[Ashi Garami]].
You can also immediately try to reap the leg, as the opponent might roll anyways.

## Keymaster

When the opponent puts his knee on your hip in order to knee cut you.
Try to pull him in, in order to make him step closer with his post leg.
When he steps closer, this will give you space to pull your bottom leg closer into keymaster guard.
When pulling your bottom leg towards you, try to follow the ground so that it doesn't get stuck.

From the Keymaster you can begin attacking the legs.
