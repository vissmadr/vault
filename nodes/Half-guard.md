## Why Half-guard?

**Connection**: Half-guard offers great connection to the opponent.
. Great connection can really compensate for disadvantages in speed and athleticism.

**Versatility**: Half-guard is incredibly versatile.
. From half-guard you can play to survive, sweep, get-up, flank, and submit.

**Synergy**: Half-guard is very synergetic and can integrate other positions.
. Can link and join together many different systems and positions.

**Kuzushi**: Half-guard is great for off-balancing the opponent.
. Being able to off-balance opponents opens up many possibilities in the position.

### Synergy

Half-guard is all about linking different systems and techniques together.

Synergetic position where individual elements work together to produce a much greater whole.
The idea of "1 + 1 = 3" fully applies here.
