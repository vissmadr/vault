# Jaguar

#empty

---

The third biggest cat.

Living in the rainforest environment.

Jaguars are excellent swimmers.
They are at home in water.

Deadly hunter relying on stealth and ambush.

They mainly attack the brain and neck.
Can also drag and even drown their prey.

Jaguars drag their killed prey inside the cover of vegetation in order to eat in peace.

Jaguars can go on periods of fasting.
But they try to hunt every few days.

Jaguars rarely attack humans, and even prefer to avoid them.


> "Less than half of their hunts are successful."
