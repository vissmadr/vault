# Lack of Anger

> "The deficiency, whether it is a certain lack of anger or something else, is
> blamed. For those who do not get angry at the things they ought are held to
> be foolish, as are those who do not get angry in the way they ought or when
> or with whom they ought. For such a person seems to lack perception and even
> not to feel pain; since he does not get angry, he seems not apt to defend
> himself against an attack. Yet to hold back in this way after having been
> treated insolently, and to overlook such treatment of one's kin, is held
> to be characteristic of a slave."
>
> Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethic
