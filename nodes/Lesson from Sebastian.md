# Lesson from Sebastian

A really good grappler I met and had the chance to roll with and talk to.

---

## Guard Passing

There are two types of guard passing, one where you constantly keep distance, disconnect, and break the grips of the opponent, and one when you go into his guard and apply pressure.
It is best to know and do both.

For guard passing, you need to be able to play against all types of guard.

When you pass you should develop multiple styles and chain them together.
Against good people the first, second, or third attack might not work, but the fourth probably will.

## Guard Play

Unlike passing, you can develop just one or two guards and have them cover your game.

## Memory and Troubleshooting

One thing I noticed is how he always thinks about what actually happened in his rolls.

Not only he remembers all of the rolls, but tries to troubleshoot them afterwards.

## Training Methodology

Asked him about the difference between the world class gyms he has been training at versus the casual gyms.

He said that the methodology is kind of the same everywhere, basically warm-ups, drills, and then sparring.
And that there are the same masses of white belts everywhere.

The difference is the people that are already in the gym 2 hours before the class starts, and 2 hours after it ends.

## Focus on Few Things

Always have a few things that you're working on simultaneously.

Doesn't need to be a single position, but not too many either.

Helps to learn BJJ, as well as avoiding burnout and just keeping it fun.
