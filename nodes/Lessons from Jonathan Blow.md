## On Self-sabotage

Don't talk your way out of success.

People love tearing other people down.

Now is the only time you can do anything.
That was true in the past, and will be true in the future.

There are always the same type of excuses.
"There is more competition now more than ever."

Everyone can find his reasons to fail.
We should find ours to succeed.

## Luck

## Money

## Graphics

Graphics really matter.
Your mind while playing the game will respond differently depending on graphics.
