# Lucky Waves

The world seems full of senses that humans cannot use, or don't know how to.
There are signals everywhere around us that we know exist, but cannot capture unless we have an appropriate device.
There are senses that some animals have, but we don't have.
There are colors that exist and some animals can see, but we cannot see them, and have no perception of them.

Imagine the possibilities when it comes to more types of sense/perception that humans, animals, and devices, are unable to percieve at all.
The possibilities are endless, and there is no way to tell how many there are, and what they do.

Imagine just for fun that concepts such as "creativity" or "luck" are actually senses that we cannot percieve, but affect us nonetheless.
