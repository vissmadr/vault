# Moving Hitbox Radius Change

Reducing the player hitbox radius while moving.

---

The idea is to reward the player while he is trying to move and dodge projectiles instead of attacking or idling.

> Don't know of other games that do it, although maybe it's just a hidden mechanic somewhere.
> I did it for my own game.
