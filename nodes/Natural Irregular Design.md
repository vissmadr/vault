# Natural Irregular Design

Intentionally avoiding perfect uniformity in order to have a more natural feel.

---

Break repetition with small, intentional imperfections and assymetries.

Small imperfections make environments feel more authentic.

Break perfect uniformity to create a more organic appearance.

Hunt for obvious repetitive patterns and vary them.

**Randomness and Noise**: Common way of achieving irregularity is replacing uniform data with [[Random Generation|Random]] and [[Pseudorandom]] approximations such as [[Noise (Procedural Generation)|Noise]].
