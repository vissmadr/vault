# Quantity for Mastery

In order to master a strategy game, you must have a vast quantity of knowledge.
That doesn't mean you need to know everything perfectly, as that's unrealistic.

You need to have excellent fundamentals, and good understanding of most things.

At the highest levels of competitive games (such as grappling or e-sports), players tend to specialize.
That specialization gives them a competitive edge, as it focuses their training towards a smaller system.
However, beyond that specialization, those same players are solid in nearly all aspects of their domain.

Specializing into a smaller and more focused system should not mean that you are to ignore everything else.
It should mean that you have become proficient in all the fundamentals, and you have now found your game.

> Good wrestlers should know every technique well, but master one or two to perfection.
> \- Bulgarian saying
