# Salience Bias

Tendency to give disproportionate attention and importance to information that stands out.

---

Vivid, unusual, emotional, or recent cases can dominate judgment even when ordinary cases are more common or relevant.

- One red apple among 49 green apples captures attention.
- A gambler’s dramatic win is more memorable than hundreds of ordinary losses.
