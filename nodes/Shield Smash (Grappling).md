# Shield Smash (Grappling)

Pressure passing position against [[Half Guard]] knee shield.

You lay on top of the opponents's knee shield, while also having a tight-waist grip.

---

**Free Arm Control**: Your free arm should probably be on his arm or his bottom leg.
. Having it on their arm prevents some of their offence and escape options, but you need to be careful about fast triangles or re-guard if they get their bottom leg out.
. Having the arm on their bottom leg prevents them from getting it out for a triangle attack or re-guard, but it does leave one of their arm free.
