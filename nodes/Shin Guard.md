# Shin Guard

#empty
#wip

---

## Setup

Whichever of the opponents legs is in front, aim for that leg.

Shin connection is foot inside, knee outside.
Grab deep with the arm and have your head tight to his leg.

Rotate a bit to the outside of the opponent knee.
This helps to prevent pressure and access to underhooks and guillotines.

The other arm and leg are free to play.
It's common for the free arm to push and rotate the opponent's knee.

## Attacks

Attacks from the Shin Guard position.

### Foot Sweep

The free arm pushes the opponent's knee in order to rotate it outward.
The free leg sweeps (big motion) the leg of the opponent from behind.
Can lean back with the Shin guard for a moment while doing this.

### Ashi Pull

Open up the space with your free arm.
Stiff arm on the opponent's far knee, aiming to bend it outward.

Drop on your shoulder towards that space, dragging his controlled leg with you.
Bring his controlled leg close, and shoot out your butterfly hook to extended through it.
Immediately after that shoot the Ashi connection.
