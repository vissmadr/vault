# Shotgun Design Case

> During playtesting, testers kept complaining that a shotgun was underpowered. The designers checked the numbers and found the opposite: statistically, it was actually a little overpowered.
> Rather than changing the weapon’s damage or other gameplay values, they changed only its sound. They gave the shotgun blast more bass, cut some of the mids, and increased the highs, making it sound much more powerful.
> They put it back in front of the testers without telling them what had changed.
> The response was essentially: “Finally! You fixed the shotgun.”
> Nothing about the weapon’s actual performance had changed. It just sounded more powerful, and that changed how powerful players perceived it to be.
