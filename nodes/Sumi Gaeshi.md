# Sumi Gaeshi

(aka. Butterfly Sweep)

#wip

---

The best sweep in grappling for me.

You can think of the SumiGaeshi as a forced forward roll.

**Core Insight**: Every SumiGaeshi is a fight against the opponent's posture, and preventing him from posting his hand on the mat.
- Posture is the relationship between the opponent's head and hips, where you want his head lower than his hips.
