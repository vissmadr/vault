# The Importance of Language

To be smarter, it's crucial that one is good at language.

---

The way humans do rational thinking seems to heavily overlap language.

Once you have a name for something, you know it more clearly.
> "We only see what we know."

The way we structure language is also related to the way we structure our thoughts.

**Children**: It's important to teach children how to speak, read, and write.
. Those skills will make them better thinkers, and greater, more capable people.

## Naming Patterns

Without a name, an undefined *thing* could be nothing more than a bypass emotion.
There is much inconsistency and fog when it comes to thinking about things we don't have named and described well.

Words and language can be used to abstractly describe just about anything that we can think of.
If you can think of something, and you recognize the pattern, you can then name it.

Once you name it, you can now call it and refer to it.
When named, that *thing* can now be called, described, and studied.
