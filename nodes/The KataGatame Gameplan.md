# The KataGatame Gameplan

#empty

Instructional by Gianni Grippo.

---

Always pull choking arm towards your own head.
Palm down.
Gable grip.
Push and rotate your head towards your opponent.

Don't drive forward!
Pull your weight back!
Finally drop your weight straight down.

Can slide from gable to butterfly grip for even more pressure.

Keep your legs close to his hips, or on top of him.
Keep close distance to your opponent's hips and don't let him use his legs.

**Mount vs Side**: Mount is probably the better finishing position.
. Side control finishes are strong in terms of pressure, but the opponent has more ways of escaping.
. Mount offers more control overall and less escape options.

Keep the mount tight to your opponent.

For that last 5% of pressure, you can windshield to the side.
From there you can also push his hips away a bit.

**Leg Fold**: From a kneeOnBelly entry, walk your weight back and then towards the opponent, trying to push his hips with your hip away from you.
From the leg fold, you can even go over and into the mount finish.

**Knee Cut Entry**: When the opponent pushes on you with a frame from your knee cut.
. You push down, he pushes back on you, you catch behind his elbow, drop down, crossface, connect the hands.
. If his frame is below your armpit, you can instead pull his elbow with your crossface hand and go for the Darce from there.
. All of this needs to be timed well with a push-pull mechanic without any telegraphing.
. From the KataGatame grip you can then go to 3/4 mount directly.
