## Far Leg Stomp

You can stomp the far leg of the opponent.
The stomp greatly widens his stance and allows for an [[Inside Ashi]] reap.

**Opponent pulls your leg**: Go for the reap anyways.
. He will probably drop and attack from a bad angle, while you attack an outside heel hook.
